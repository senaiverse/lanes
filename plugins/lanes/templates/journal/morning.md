# morning

Questions for the human, queued while away mode is on (`lanes away on`). When the human is back, the lead asks them
one at a time through the ask tool, records each answer where it belongs, and deletes the entry.

## Entry form

```
### <short question>
- Asked by: <lane or lead>, <date>
- Facts: <what is known, with file:line or links>
- Options and costs: <A: cost>; <B: cost>
- Recommended: <option>, because <reason>
- Blocks: <what waits on the answer, and what continues meanwhile>
```
